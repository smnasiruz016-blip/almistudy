How much money do I need to study in Canada?

Applicants need to show the first year's tuition fees, travel costs to and from Canada, and at least CAD 20,635 for cost of living in 2024 (excluding Quebec). The cost-of-living amount is separate from tuition and travel costs. Source: ImmiLaw Global — its own page · checked 2026-11-19
