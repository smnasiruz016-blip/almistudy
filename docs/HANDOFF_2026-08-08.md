## The lesson of this pass: SLUG similarity is the wrong instrument

The ≥0.8 full-slug scan — the metric that has carried the last four passes — found only
**6 of the 25** duplicates. It missed:

- `inha-university-in-tashkent` vs live `inha-university-tashkent` — one extra token
  scores 3/4 = 0.75, just under the threshold.
- `mdis-in-tashkent` vs live `management-development-institute-of-singapore-tashkent` —
  an acronym slug shares almost no tokens with a spelled-out one.
- `azerbaijan-state-university-of-economics-unec` vs live
  `azerbaijan-state-economic-university` — "economics" ≠ "economic", so 3/7 = 0.43.
- the whole Oman cluster (`college-banking-financial-studies` vs
  `college-of-banking-and-financial-studies`, and four more) — stopword-stripped slugs.

A token-containment scan caught more but produced **70 pairs for ~15 real defects**,
because `azerbaijan-university` is a subset of nearly every Azerbaijani name.

**What worked: comparing normalised institution NAMES** — parentheticals and native script
stripped, `of/and/the/in/named/after/university/state/national` dropped — at ≥0.6 within
country, with city shown alongside. That surfaced all 25 with very few false positives.
**Once a converter is free to rewrite slugs, the slug stops being the identity and the
name is.** Fold the name scan into the standing pre-flight.
